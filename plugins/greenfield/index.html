Greenfield

A planner and an implementer for building features with Claude and Codex. You decide what to build, one agent writes the code, and a reviewer on the other model family checks it.

Planning

The planner stays in discussion until you ask it to move on. Each step is a document in the work's bundle, revised in place as you learn.

explain

“How does this work?” Answers inline, or writes an explainer page for bigger topics. Ends with questions, never with a build.

brief

What is wrong or wanted, for whom, and how you would know it is solved. It contains no solution.

options

Two or three real options, plus the smallest version and doing nothing, each with its complexity and trade-offs. Socrates challenges them once before you pick.

spike

One time-boxed question, when a decision is blocked on a fact nobody has.

mockup

Three interface options per round until you approve one. It becomes the design reference.

plan

One HTML cover sheet: scope, locked decisions, package cards, and How we will know it works. You approve it.

Helpers the planner can call, each with one question and a fresh context: investigator (code and logs), researcher (outside sources), socrates (challenges the options), and mockup-artist (images, Claude planner only).

Handing off. When you approve a cover sheet, the planner gives you the agent-farm run greenfield/implementer command. You launch it yourself and pick the model. Under an orchestrator, the orchestrator launches it for you.

Implementation

One agent writes all the code, tests and fixes. Its helpers only read, verify and review.

  1. Preflight. Checks commands, the app, test accounts, and the reviewer launcher before relying on them.
  2. Stages. Builds each package from the cover sheet, then checks the diff, the full caller and data path, and focused tests.
  3. Frontend verification. As UI becomes runnable, the verifier drives the named journeys and reports evidence. The implementer makes every fix.
  4. Whole-feature checks. Every journey and check in How we will know it works needs evidence on the current code.
  5. Draft PR and review. One final review by a model from the other family, then focused follow-ups on its must-fix findings.
  6. Wrap-up. Clean-up, a post-mortem page, and a session trace in the bundle. The PR stays a draft until you merge it.

Review modes

  • single is the default: one reviewer.
  • dual adds the second reviewer. It needs your explicit approval.
  • none skips review. It needs your approval, except for small, low-risk changes such as copy or docs, which may skip with the reason stated up front.

Bugs

The bug-reporter restates the symptom, reproduces it with qa and investigator, ranks a few hypotheses, and writes a report with a clear “gone when” check. Then it picks a route:

  • direct-to-implementer: obvious cause, contained change. Launch the implementer with the report as its source.
  • options-first: fixing it needs a design choice. Take the report to the planner.
  • cannot-reproduce: it says what was tried and what evidence would help.

Coordinating many items

The orchestrator follows the host's workspace and session mechanics. It triages each item, launches the planner, bug-reporter or implementer (with the variant you picked) in its own workspace, relays your decisions, and keeps a status board. It waits for events instead of polling. It never implements and never merges.

What you get

Every work item gets one bundle folder, local by default (tmp/greenfield/<slug>/) or published where you choose:

index.htmlthe brief, and the hub linking everything else
options.htmlthe options you picked from
cover-sheet.htmlthe approved plan, read by you, the implementer and the reviewers
trace.htmlthe conversation, with tool calls collapsed
post-mortem.htmlhow implementation went, where it got stuck, what was verified
explainers/, mockups/, evidence/supporting pages, designs, screenshots
Rules that always hold. Moving from explanation to plan to implementation is your decision. One writer per workspace. A check that could not run counts as unproven. Nothing is merged for you.
