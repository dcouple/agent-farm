Building: ideate, implementer, review, QA

ideate

Talks the idea through and pushes back, offers mockups for anything with a screen, and researches when it matters. When you call it ready, Socrates reviews the premise. It creates the GitHub issue or Grain brief only with your go-ahead, then stops.

implementer

Plans the ticket, has Luna workers write the code, opens a draft PR, then runs the review lanes in parallel and works through one fix queue. It ends with a final review of the whole PR and never merges.

reviewer

Runs 15 review angles in parallel and ranks findings as Must-Fix, Should-Fix or Suggestion. By default you approve fixes first; “fix everything” runs the whole loop.

qa-and-fix

Tests the PR, finds each failure's root cause, and fixes only small, safe bugs within the PR's scope, as separate commits. It reports either “tested and ready” or what still needs you.

Implementer review lanes

After the draft PR, these run at the same time: checks and CI, end-to-end QA on Sol, the PR description, correctness, integration and security, intent and test coverage, and bot and human feedback. Low-risk changes like copy, translations, docs or simple config skip the focused reviews, and QA stays off unless you ask. For everything else, the implementer asks whether to run end-to-end QA and starts it after 60 seconds if you don't answer.

Other jobs

  • product-researcher picks a research method (deep dive, tech deep dive, product comparison and others), has a cold reader check the write-up, and saves it to Grain. It writes no code.
  • business goes through context, adversarial research, discussion, a reviewed spec, a reviewed draft and release. Each stage runs in a fresh context and files go under .business/. Preparing a document never means sending it.
  • seo starts new sites with foundations, otherwise briefing and content strategy. You approve the strategy before any writing starts, and data stays in .seo/.
  • audits reports what to close or update, with evidence from the default branch. It closes issues or PRs only for the rows you approve and opens one PR for doc, generated-file and CI fixes.
  • raw is the model on its own, with a few good habits: investigate bugs, test first, prepare the PR, watch CI, and get a review from the other vendor's model before calling a PR done.
Rules that always hold. Nothing is merged for you. Irreversible, production or outward-facing actions wait for your approval. Evidence like QA screenshots stays out of the repository.
